Suicide Awareness

Talking about suicide doesn’t cause it. Understanding warning signs and knowing how to respond can save lives.

❤️ Why This Matters

Suicide is preventable. Understanding the facts is where prevention starts...

If you’re thinking about suicide right now

Please reach out. Call or text 988. You don’t have to face this alone.

Suicide is a leading cause of death among adolescents. Wyoming’s rate is consistently among the highest in the nation.

But suicide is preventable. Most people who are suicidal want the pain to stop, not their life to end. Talking about suicide does not cause it — silence is more dangerous than conversation.

Wyoming Connection

168 suicide deaths in 2024. 72% involve firearms. Long distances to crisis centers make means safety especially critical here.

Sudden calmness after depression is one of the most misunderstood signs. It can mean the person has made a decision and feels relief — not that they’ve recovered.

🤝 Risk, Protective Factors & How to Respond

What increases risk, what reduces it, and what to do...

Risk factors: depression, substance use, previous self-harm, access to lethal means, feeling like a burden.

Protective factors: strong connections, sense of belonging, safe storage of firearms and medications, access to care. These reduce risk but don’t eliminate it.

It is safe to ask directly: "Are you thinking about suicide?" Research shows asking does not increase risk.

If someone is at immediate risk:

  1. Stay with them
  2. Remove access to lethal means if safe
  3. Get help — Call 988, text 741741, or call 911
  4. Don’t promise secrecy

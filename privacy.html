Privacy, Compliance & Sources

Information for educators, administrators, and parents about WYMind's commitment to student safety, privacy, and research-backed content.

Our Mission

To increase mental health literacy among Wyoming students by transforming mental health concepts into relatable and meaningful education, empowering students to recognize warning signs, support their friends, and navigate their own well-being.

A student-built initiative for Wyoming adolescents

Terms of Service

What WYMind Is

WYMind is a student-built educational platform designed to improve mental health literacy among Wyoming adolescents. Our purpose is to help young people understand mental health topics, recognize signs of common conditions, support their peers, and know when and how to seek professional help.

What WYMind Is Not

WYMind is not a clinical tool. We do not diagnose mental health conditions, provide treatment recommendations, or replace professional mental health services. The information on this site is educational in nature and intended to be a starting point for understanding — not a substitute for consultation with qualified mental health professionals.

Key Terms

  • Educational Purpose Only: All content is designed for mental health literacy, not diagnosis or treatment
  • Guidance, Not Answers: We provide information to help users understand their experiences and have better conversations with trusted adults — we do not provide definitive clinical conclusions
  • Professional Help: We consistently encourage users to speak with school counselors, mental health professionals, or trusted adults for personalized support
  • Crisis Resources: We prominently display crisis resources (988, Crisis Text Line) throughout the site for anyone who needs immediate help
  • No Liability: WYMind and its creator(s) are not liable for decisions made based on the educational content provided

Trusted Adult Integration

WYMind is designed to work alongside — not replace — the adults in a student's life. Throughout our content, we encourage users to connect with trusted adults such as parents, teachers, school counselors, coaches, and mentors. We believe that mental health literacy is most effective when it supports, rather than bypasses, existing support systems.

Privacy Policy

Zero Data Collection

WYMind is committed to student privacy. Our site operates with a zero data collection policy:

  • We do not require user accounts or logins
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
  • We do not use cookies for tracking purposes
  • We do not collect IP addresses or device information
  • We do not share any data with third parties
  • We do not use analytics that identify individual users

Users can access all content anonymously. There is nothing to delete because we never collect anything in the first place.

Regulatory Compliance

WYMind is designed to meet or exceed the requirements of federal student privacy regulations:

FERPA Compliant

The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act protects student education records. Because WYMind collects no student data whatsoever, there are no education records to protect — we are compliant by design.

COPPA Compliant

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act requires parental consent before collecting personal information from children under 13. Because WYMind collects no personal information from users of any age, COPPA requirements are automatically satisfied.

School Adoption Readiness

WYMind was designed with school adoption in mind. Administrators and IT departments can deploy WYMind knowing that:

  • No student data is collected, stored, or at risk
  • No parental consent forms are required for data collection (because there is none)
  • No integration with student information systems is needed
  • The site can be accessed on school networks without privacy concerns
  • All external links (MHA screening, crisis resources) go to established, reputable organizations

About WYMind

WYMind was created by a high school student at Kelly Walsh High School in Casper, Wyoming. The project grew from a recognition that Wyoming faces unique mental health challenges — including one of the highest suicide rates in the nation — and that many adolescents lack basic mental health literacy.

This is not a commercial product. WYMind is a student initiative built to serve the community. The creator has no financial interest in the site and receives no compensation for its use.

The project has been reviewed by school counselors and is designed to complement, not replace, existing school mental health resources and SEL curriculum.
